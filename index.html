Welcome

Currently, I’m pursuing my master’s in Data Science at NYU’s Center for Data Science, supervised by Prof. Umang Bhatt, researching trustworthy ML. My current interests include orchestrating multiple agents and developing methods to detect skill atrophy in sequential decision-making tasks. I previously worked as a Technical Product Manager at Flixstock, an e-commerce startup, leading generative AI solutions to revamp image catalogs and enhance online image editing tools using AI. Prior to that, I led AI research at Standard Chartered Bank under the direction of Amarendra Rout, focusing on improving predictive modeling with time-series data from credit card transactions and evaluating the performance of third-party bias and drift detection tools. I also collaborated on developing an LLM-Optimized Relationship Manager Portal featuring adaptive query resolution, fine-tuned retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and intent classification. I’m a graduate of IIT BHU with a BTech, where I completed coursework in differential equations and linear algebra, independently worked on computer vision-based image recognition projects. Outside of work, I enjoy writing and creating music. I served as the secretary of the Western Music Club at IIT BHU and performed in over 10 events across the country during my four years there. I also love playing racket sports—whether it’s badminton, tennis, or, more recently, pickleball—and I’m always up for a game.
