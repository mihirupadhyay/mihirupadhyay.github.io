Mihir Upadhyay

New York University

I'm pursuing my Master's in Data Science at the Center for Data Science at New York University. Currently, I'm working under the supervision of Prof. Umang Bhatt. My research focuses on developing algorithms that enhance human-AI collaboration, particularly in education and security contexts.

Recently, I interned with Prof. Muhammad Bilal Zafar at Research Center Trustworthy Data Science and Security Ruhr University Bochum. There, I systematically evaluated the diversity of information presented by GenAI content retrieval agents, comparing the information diversity in deployed GenAI tools against traditional search systems.

Prior to my academic pursuits, I ventured into industry as a Product Manager at Flixstock. Working under Harinder Keer and Ashish Kumar, I researched AI-based image generation using diffusion models and GANs.

Before Flixstock, I was part of the AI tools team at Standard Chartered, working with Thekkath Sabarinath and Amarendra Rout. My research there centered on AI tools implementation (like Truera and DataRobot) in the banking sector.

I completed my Bachelor's degree at IIT BHU, where I developed a passion for data science and machine learning through participating in Kaggle competitions, hackathons, and competitive programming challenges.

Beyond my professional life, you'll find me immersed in music – whether jamming at home or discovering new playlists in cozy cafés. I enjoy singing and occasionally channel my inspiration into writing.
